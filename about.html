About

I’m Izrahiah Immanuel, a mechanical engineer who graduated from Georgia Tech with High Honors and a Registered Engineer in Training (EIT). I pair engineering rigor with a strong creative streak: I also hold a second degree in architecture, built a fighting game from scratch in Godot, and sell art commissions on the side.

For my capstone, I designed and prototyped a shock absorber test rig for college racing teams at 15% of market cost. As a prototyping instructor at the Invention Studio at Georgia Tech, I trained students on waterjets and manual mills and helped them bring their own projects to life.

My work spans CAD, simulation, and CFD in Siemens NX and Fusion 360 alongside architectural design, from urban agriculture and coastal community buildings to multifamily housing. I’m always interested in new collaborations and conversation, so feel free to connect with me on LinkedIn.
