For my capstone, I designed and prototyped a low-cost shock absorber test rig that lets college racing teams measure damper performance. I created the Fusion 360 assemblies and drawings for the fabrication package, which cut production cost to 15% of the market rate. I also led our multidisciplinary team, setting goals and accountability metrics for each member.
