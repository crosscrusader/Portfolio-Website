Mechanical Engineering · Project 01

Regera Model and Simulation

YearFall 2025
SoftwareSiemens NX
TeamIzrahiah Immanuel, Parth Patel, Jonathan Vollrath

This project is a parametric CAD model and simulation study of the Koenigsegg Regera, a 1,500-horsepower hybrid grand tourer with only 80 units produced from 2015 to 2022. Working as a team of three in Siemens NX, we built the model from reference images, scaling them to one another and aligning them to the datum of the main assembly. We traced modeling curves over the images and built the body as a full surface model using lofted, curve mesh, fill, ruled, and studio surfaces, then sewed and thickened the surfaces into solid bodies. The wheels follow the car’s staggered setup of 19-inch wheels in front and 20-inch wheels in the rear, and all the parts came together in a full model assembly. We then animated the hood, rear deck, and doors with cylindrical joints and polynomial drivers to recreate the Regera’s “Autoskin” hydraulics. Using the finished model, we ran a CFD wind tunnel study at the car’s 250 mph top speed to find its drag coefficient, which published sources put between 0.32 and 0.37. We also ran an impact study on the laminated glass windshield and a linear static study of how far the rear deck wing deflects under the car’s 992 lb of downforce at 155 mph.
